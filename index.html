Bluetooth isn't available here

Sending to the watch uses Web Bluetooth. It works in:

  • Chrome on Android (recommended, since your phone is already next to the watch)
  • Chrome or Edge on Windows, macOS, Linux or ChromeOS

It does not work on iPhone/iPad or Firefox. The page must also be opened over https:// (or localhost). You can still design faces and Download .watch here, then send from another device.

Before you connect

  1. Free the watch. It talks to only one app at a time. On your phone, force-stop NoiseFit (Settings → Apps → NoiseFit → Force stop). If you're sending from a PC, turn the phone's Bluetooth off.
  2. Keep the watch awake and close to this device.
  3. Press Connect and pick your watch from the list.

How it works

          
A Noise face is a single cfg_res.watch file. It holds:

          
    
            
  • a JSON layout that sets where the watch draws the time and date, and in what colour,
    • 
            
  • a 240×280 background in RGB565,
    • 
            
  • a 184×210 thumbnail for the face picker.
    • 
          

          
The file isn't encrypted or signed. This app builds it in your browser and sends it over Bluetooth Low Energy with
             the same packet sequence NoiseFit uses: 241-byte chunks, an acknowledgement every 10 chunks, then a byte-sum and MD5 check.

          
What you can change

          
  • Background art (any photo or preset), clock and date position and colour, and baked-in text or graphics.

          
What you can't change (yet)

          
    
            
  • The digit font and analog hands. The watch draws these from its firmware.
    • 
            
  • Menus, notifications and settings screens. These live in the firmware, not the face file.
    • 
          

          
Privacy

          
Everything runs locally in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

          
Not affiliated with Noise / Nexxbase. Use at your own risk. Re-installing any face from NoiseFit restores the stock look.
